# Frostrail Wiki

[Frostrail Wiki](https://frostrail.org/) is an independent, player-focused guide to Frostrail, the dieselpunk survival-crafting FPS from FakeFish and Shiro Unlimited. Frostrail sends one to four players north through a frozen empire aboard the Eden Engine, an armored train that serves as transport, shelter, workshop, storage, and the crew's shared lifeline.

The wiki follows the game through Closed Beta and its planned Q4 2026 Steam Early Access release. Information that may change between builds is dated, while unannounced values are left open instead of being filled with estimates.

## What Makes Frostrail Different

- **A moving train base:** Fuel, power, heat, storage, crafting stations, carriages, upgrades, and defense all come back to the Eden Engine.
- **Survival beyond the rails:** Expeditions into tundra, settlements, bunkers, ruins, and dungeons expose the crew to cold, limited supplies, and hostile creatures.
- **Solo and online co-op:** Frostrail supports one to four players, allowing a crew to divide train work, scavenging, navigation, and defense without fixed classes.
- **Crafting and modular weapons:** Recovered resources support refining, repair, train expansion, and modular firearm assembly.
- **A dangerous journey north:** Each level moves the expedition deeper into a colder and more demanding frozen world.

## Popular Player Guides

### Surviving the First Expedition

Treat the Eden Engine as the center of every plan. Leave the train with a clear objective, watch warmth and ammunition together, keep the return route understandable, and bring back supplies that support the next leg of the journey. A longer expedition is not automatically a better one if it leaves the crew without fuel, equipment, or time to recover.

### Running the Eden Engine

The train is more than a vehicle. It provides heat, carries workstations and storage, pulls additional carriages, and creates a failure condition the entire crew must respect. Fuel, power demand, pulling weight, structural upgrades, and defense should be considered as one connected survival system.

### Weapons and Crafting

Frostrail uses ranged combat, valuable ammunition, basic melee options, and modular firearm parts. The wiki distinguishes named weapons from broader weapon families and keeps screenshot values tied to the build in which they appeared. Check the page date before planning around an exact recipe or damage number.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is Frostrail coming out?

The Steam store lists Frostrail for Early Access in Q4 2026. A specific release day and price have not been announced.

### Is Frostrail multiplayer?

Yes. Frostrail supports solo play and online co-op for one to four players. Cross-play, split-screen, matchmaking, and server details have not been announced.

### Is Frostrail coming to PS5 or Xbox?

Frostrail is announced for PC and Steam first. The developers have said console versions may be evaluated for version 1.0 or later, but no PlayStation, Xbox, or Nintendo edition is currently announced.

### How do I join the Closed Beta?

Register through Frostrail's official Steam page and announcement links. Registration does not guarantee selection, and invitations or downloads offered through unrelated sellers, direct messages, or file hosts should not be trusted.

### Is Frostrail connected to Barotrauma?

Frostrail is developed by FakeFish, the studio behind Barotrauma, and carries a similar interest in co-op systems and crew pressure. No shared fictional universe has been announced.
